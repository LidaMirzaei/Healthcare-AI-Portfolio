# AI for Cancer Drug Discovery — Start Here

**Start:** October 16, 2026. **Target review:** April 16, 2027.

## Goal
Develop research-grade introductory skills in **computational oncology drug discovery**: predicting molecular bioactivity, modeling drug combinations, studying molecular graphs and learning the basics of targeted therapeutics and antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs).

This is **not** drug manufacturing quality assurance, packaging defect inspection, or clinical survival prediction. Those older folders are historical exploratory tracks, not the current objective.

## Scientific boundaries
Model rankings are hypotheses, **not evidence that a therapy is effective or safe in people**. We will not mix pills, recommend combinations to patients, claim a new cure, or use restricted/confidential experimental data. Any claim needs proper independent experimental and clinical validation.

## Week 1 (Oct 16–22): first steps
1. Refresh Python functions, lists, dictionaries and pandas.
2. Learn the difference between a target, a ligand, a molecule and an assay.
3. Learn what a SMILES molecular representation is.
4. Study one example record in ChEMBL and identify its target, measurement and assay type.
5. Record a glossary and short notebook explanation in GitHub.

Useful sources:

## Research standards
Track data licenses and citations, use independent test sets, inspect missingness and label noise, prevent molecular scaffold / cell-line leakage, compare simple baselines before deep networks and report uncertainty and limitations.
