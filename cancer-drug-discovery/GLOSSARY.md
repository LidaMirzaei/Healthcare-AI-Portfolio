# Working Glossary

- **Drug target:** a biological molecule or pathway whose modulation may change disease behavior.
- **Ligand:** a molecule that binds a target.
- **SMILES:** text representation of molecular structure.
- **Bioactivity assay:** an experimental measurement of biological response.
- **IC50:** concentration producing 50% inhibition under an assay's specified conditions.
- **Synergy:** a combination response exceeding a stated reference model's expectation; definitions depend on the model.
- **ADC:** antibody–drug conjugate, incorporating an antibody, linker and payload.
- **Cell line:** cells maintained for experimental research; results do not equal human clinical outcomes.
