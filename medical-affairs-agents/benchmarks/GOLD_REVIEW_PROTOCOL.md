# Expert-adjudicated breast ADC reference dataset: review protocol

**Status: ACTIVE REVIEW PREPARATION — NOT A COMPLETED GOLD STANDARD**

## What is in place now

- Ten **different parent study families** in the existing preliminary [ADC reference registry](ADC_REFERENCE_CANDIDATES_V1.json), all original PMID+DOI identities publicly checked.
- Ten source-linked, **unapproved** candidate outcome records in `reference_review_queue.json` generated by [clinical_reference_review.py](../clinical_reference_review.py).
- **Thirty mandatory additional review tasks**: harms/grade, study design and outcome-specific bias, and missing or negative results for each study.
- Two completely **blank** submission forms for two *different*, medically qualified reviewers. They are not filled in and do not imply that reviewers are assigned.
- Six other parent studies in [RESERVED_V4_TRIAL_FAMILIES.json](RESERVED_V4_TRIAL_FAMILIES.json), reserved for a later evaluation and not yet scored.
- Strict [v2 clinical-context evaluator](../clinical_gold_evaluator_v2.py) for use **only after** real independent adjudication; supports original PubMed-only publications, 95.01% confidence intervals and single-arm response percentages.
- External medical expertise, publisher permissions and actual credential verification are **not provided by this repository**.

## First review round: how to do it

1. A project owner appoints **two different qualified oncology/Medical Affairs research reviewers** and separately confirms their qualifications and independence. Personal credentials or corporate files must not go into the public repository. Note: two invented reviewer IDs do not prove that two human reviewers exist.
2. Each reviewer independently receives the same version of the candidate set (check `candidate_set_sha256`). Each sees the **original published paper, trial registry, protocol/statistical analysis plan, corrections, appendices and safety data**, with legally approved access. The previous machine-extracted result is a *proposal*, not an answer.
3. Both independently assess each endpoint's source, exact population and biomarker definition, experimental/comparator arms, sample sizes and analysis denominator, endpoint hierarchy, effect measure, CI level and bounds, cutoff date, concomitant and subsequent therapies, missingness, harms and limitations. CONSORT 2025 identifies required trial-reporting information but **does not act as a trial-quality score**.
4. For each of ten initial candidate claims, the reviewer records `supported`, `unsupported` or `unverifiable`. To say `supported`, all required source, context, safety and methods fields must be filled. Any missing CI, unresolved cutoff, missing source table, unclear comparator, or open correction is a review gap, not an inferred fact.
5. Reviewers also enumerate **additional relevant outcomes not in the seed** (including final OS and serious safety). An initial list with only one outcome per study is **not sufficiently exhaustive** for a true recall benchmark.
6. Run `clinical_reference_review.py compare` on the two separate reviewer sheets. All differing interpretations or values become **explicit conflicts**. Even full agreement yields **zero automatic gold claims** because human identity, competence and original-source verification cannot be authenticated by this public script.
7. Appoint an independent senior adjudicator to resolve disputes from the primary sources, noting decision, uncertainty and each evidence locator. A qualified organization then signs off on the adjudicated corpus **outside this public GitHub workflow**.
8. Store the real adjudicated set privately. Freeze the exact JSON and evaluation code commit/dataset digest. **Only then** use `clinical_gold_evaluator_v2.py` against the completely separate, untrained studies in the reserved family pool.

## Source and rights rules

- A PMC or publisher link is **not** automatically permission to reprocess full-text in a commercial AI system. Article license and institutional text-mining rights are checked separately.
- Public GitHub stores open-source code, metadata, consent-safe reviews and non-proprietary source references — **not** licensed article text, private notes, confidential documents or reviewer identity details.
- Specific-source locators such as `Results / Table 2 / Figure 3` are necessary. A paper-wide citation alone cannot support all conclusions. Note the corresponding article version and any corrections.
- Subgroup results, post-hoc studies, follow-up OS papers and interim analyses from the **same NCT** must not be treated as independent trials.

For two **actually completed** reviewer submissions in a private approved workspace:

```bash
python medical-affairs-agents/clinical_reference_review.py compare \
  --queue path/to/reference_review_queue.json \
  --first path/to/independent_reviewer_A.json \
  --second path/to/independent_reviewer_B.json \
  --out path/to/disagreements_not_gold.json
```

The comparator refuses blank, duplicate, incomplete or not independently attested reviews.

## End-to-end evaluation — only after signoff

- Gold must include all relevant reported eligible outcomes, including null/negative efficacy and serious adverse events. Otherwise recall will be spuriously high.
- Freeze training/development and holdout **by underlying trial family**, not by publication. Do not tune after looking at heldout results.
- Measure strict matched endpoint/arm/population/cutoff/source citation; separately score numerical tuples, unsupported claims, abstentions (and missed relevant outcomes), harm omissions, false claims, and time spent by expert reviewers.
- Public automation cannot verify the real-world identity of two reviewers; the v2 evaluator's boolean/reference gate is **structural only**, and its output does not certify medical safety or clinical accuracy.
