**Development review queue only — not medically adjudicated or approved.**

Results, supplementary safety-table and cutoff verification. Do not infer
between-trial comparative safety from the percentages in different cohorts.

### Scientific review gates

- Verify exact analysis populations, denominators, grade, causality attribution, data cutoffs, table source, and other adverse events.
- Separate primary, subgroup, exploratory and final outcomes. In BEGONIA arm 6, 62.1% ORR did **not** satisfy the prespecified response target.
- Confirm missing safety outcomes and trial registry updates; check benefit and harms together.
- Determine whether source material may legally be processed in the intended commercial or public environment.
- Two qualified reviewers must independently validate any safety statement intended for external use.

This page is a **human-review priority tracker**, not a validated clinical comparison, and it must not be used to compute a cross-trial safety ranking. No paid API or corporate data was used.
