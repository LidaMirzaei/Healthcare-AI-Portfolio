# TROPION-Breast01: Evidence Brief for Medical Affairs (October 2026)

**Purpose:** Independent public-literature research demonstration, **not** a medical recommendation, regulatory submission, promotional claim, or Daiichi Sankyo approved content.

**Scope:** Three full-text-accessible articles focused on **the same phase III clinical trial (NCT05104866)**, plus one essential open-access final OS publication from 2026 for context. **Do not count these as independent trials or pool their patient populations.**

## Two questions answered
**Q1 — Efficacy:** For adults with previously treated inoperable/metastatic HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer, what evidence supports datopotamab deruxtecan (Dato-DXd) versus investigator's choice of single-agent chemotherapy (ICC), and what remains uncertain about overall survival?

**Q2 — Benefit-risk and applicability:** What toxicity, patient-reported outcomes and geographic-subgroup limitations affect interpretation?

## Population and design
- Adult patients with inoperable/metastatic **HR+/HER2−** breast cancer after progression on endocrine therapy, further endocrine therapy unsuitable, and one or two prior chemotherapy lines in the advanced setting.
- Global international phase III, randomized 1:1, open-label trial; Dato-DXd **6 mg/kg every 3 weeks** versus investigator choice of eribulin, capecitabine, vinorelbine or gemcitabine.
- Dual primary endpoints: **PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR)** and **OS**. Secondary outcomes include ORR, safety and patient-reported measures.
- Important: HER2-negative and HER2-positive are different disease groups. These findings do not generalize to HER2-positive early-stage disease.

## Scientific appraisal: strengths
1. **Randomized controlled phase III design** reduces baseline confounding compared with retrospective series; the trial's large global sample enables more informative global estimates. [Primary trial Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11771365/)
2. **BICR for PFS** helps reduce outcome-assessment bias despite open-label treatment. [Primary trial Methods/Efficacy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11771365/)
3. **Mature OS publication** addresses the crucial survival endpoint rather than relying on the earlier immature trend. [Final OS report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41448362/)
4. **Independent ILD adjudication and prespecified PRO instruments** provide more structured harms and patient-experience assessment. [Safety/PRO Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13449377/)
5. **Prespecified China analysis** adds regional descriptive evidence. It does **not** represent an independent replication. [China Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13583573/)

## Scientific appraisal: limitations and risks of overinterpretation
1. **OS was not statistically improved at the final analysis.** The observed PFS gain must not be translated into a survival benefit. Imbalanced use of subsequent ADCs (12.3% Dato-DXd vs 24.0% ICC) is a possible explanation, not proof of why OS was null. [Final OS paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41448362/)
2. **Open-label trial:** knowledge of treatment may influence symptoms, reporting and some secondary outcomes. BICR provides partial mitigation specifically for PFS.
3. **Control-arm heterogeneity:** investigator-selected chemotherapies may vary in tolerability and effectiveness; conclusions apply to the studied ICC mix.
4. **Safety tradeoffs:** fewer grade ≥3 TRAEs in the initial comparison (20.8% vs 44.7%) coexist with clinically relevant stomatitis, ocular surface events and occasional ILD including a reported fatal case; do not present 'safer' without outcome-by-outcome context. [Primary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11771365/) / [Safety](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13449377/)
5. **PROs:** endpoints vary in strength (secondary versus exploratory); completion is ~82.5% at baseline and falls over time. Missingness can bias QoL analyses; detailed threshold assumptions and supplements need review. [PRO Methods/Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13449377/)
6. **China cohort:** 83 patients, exploratory nominal P values and relatively wide intervals. Baseline prior CDK4/6 treatment differed numerically (77.3% vs 61.5%). The 0.54 PFS HR must not be interpreted as demonstrating China-specific superiority to the global HR 0.63. [China Methods/Results/Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13583573/)
7. **Publication dependence:** 2025/2026 papers analyze overlapping people from NCT05104866, so claiming "three separate studies proved efficacy" would be double/triple counting.
8. **Evidence integrity:** table values, appendices, protocol amendments, missing-data analyses, risk-of-bias domains, competing interests, and current regulatory label require dedicated full-text/supplement review before any formal external use.

## Executive interpretation (for research screening, not prescribing)
**Answer to Q1:** In this randomized phase III population, Dato-DXd improved independently assessed progression-free survival against the studied chemotherapy comparators. **Final overall survival did not significantly differ**. This distinction must appear at the top of any executive brief.

**Answer to Q2:** The benefit-risk picture is not a single number. Severe treatment-related toxicity was less frequent in the original comparison, but oral, ocular and pulmonary risks are important. Patient-reported functioning and selected symptoms deteriorated later on Dato-DXd, with methodological limitations. A regional subgroup provides consistency signals but is too small for definitive geographic subgroup effects.

### Suggested reader-facing questions
- Is the decision about first-line or later-line disease? This evidence applies to **previously treated** advanced HR+/HER2− disease.
- Are stakeholders asking about PFS, OS, QoL, or specific adverse events? Report these separately.
- Does any document mistakenly count these publications as distinct patient cohorts?
- Have safety supplements, corrections, current jurisdictional labels and investigator conflicts been checked?
